Private, on-device TEAM evaluations

Eval IQ helps Tennessee school evaluators run TEAM classroom observations from start to finish on iPhone, iPad, and Mac: plan the year from each educator's license and prior-year LOE, script lessons live, upload evidence, score with the rubric at hand, and hold the post-conference.

Everything stays on your device. No account, no server, no analytics.
